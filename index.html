SMS Assistant

Text message coordination program · Operated by Peter Nielson, sole proprietor

SMS Assistant is a text message (SMS) coordination program operated by Peter Nielson, an individual volunteer coordinator, for small local groups he personally participates in — currently a religious congregation (the Seattle Hill Ward, including its Elders Quorum) and a family group. Messages are informational and coordination only: event reminders, RSVP requests, volunteer sign-ups, carpool coordination, and scheduling notifications. SMS Assistant does not send marketing or promotional messages.

Every message identifies the group it relates to — for example, a message to the congregation group begins with Seattle Hill Ward.

How to join

Text JOIN to (425) 534-5348

to receive coordination text messages from your group

By texting JOIN you agree to receive informational and coordination text messages from SMS Assistant. Msg frequency varies. Msg & data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out, HELP for help. See our Terms & Conditions and Privacy Policy.

Texting the program number is the only way to join. We never add a phone number to the program on someone's behalf, and we never buy, rent, upload, or import phone numbers. See the full text-to-join details.
