Join by text message

This is the exact call to action used on printed handouts and in spoken announcements for SMS Assistant groups. Texting the program number is the only way to join.

The call to action

Text JOIN to (425) 534-5348

to receive coordination text messages from your group

By texting JOIN you agree to receive informational and coordination text messages from SMS Assistant. Msg frequency varies. Msg & data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out, HELP for help. See our Terms & Conditions and Privacy Policy.

Consent is optional. It is not required to attend activities or to be a member of any group.

What happens when you text JOIN

  1. You text JOIN to (425) 534-5348 from the mobile number you want messages sent to. You may add a group code after the keyword if you know it — for example, JOIN EQ for the Elders Quorum group.
  2. If you did not include a group code, you receive a reply asking which group you are joining.
  3. You receive a reply asking for your name, so messages can be addressed to you and RSVPs can be recorded.
  4. Once you reply with your name, you receive a subscription confirmation naming the program, stating that message frequency varies, that message and data rates may apply, and how to reply HELP for help and STOP to cancel.
  5. You then receive coordination messages for your group — event reminders, RSVP requests, volunteer sign-ups, carpool coordination, and scheduling notifications.

How we know the number publicly

The program number is publicized to congregation members through announcements at in-person meetings, printed handouts distributed at those meetings (see the printable handout), and this website.

No message is ever sent to a number that has not first texted this number to opt in. Numbers are never uploaded, purchased, imported, or enrolled by an administrator. Your inbound text is kept as the record of your consent.

Keywords

  • Join: JOIN, START, UNSTOP, YES
  • Cancel: STOP, STOPALL, UNSUBSCRIBE, CANCEL, END, QUIT
  • Help: HELP, INFO

Stopping messages and getting help

Reply STOP to any message to unsubscribe. Opt-out is processed automatically and immediately. You will receive one confirmation and then no further messages, unless you sign up again. Reply HELP for help, or contact us at nielsonpa@gmail.com or 801-921-9714.

Message and data rates may apply. Message frequency varies depending on group activities. Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.
