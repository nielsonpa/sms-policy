This is the exact call-to-action used on printed handouts and in spoken announcements for Personal Assistant Network (PAN) groups.

The call to action

Text JOIN to (425) 534-5348

to receive coordination text messages from your group

By texting JOIN you agree to receive informational and coordination text messages from Personal Assistant Network (PAN). Msg frequency varies. Msg & data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out, HELP for help. See our Terms & Conditions and Privacy Policy.

Consent is optional. It is not required to attend activities or to be a member of any group.

What happens when you text JOIN

  1. You text JOIN to (425) 534-5348 from the mobile number you want messages sent to.
  2. You receive one confirmation message telling you that you are subscribed, along with message frequency and rate disclosures and how to opt out.
  3. You then receive coordination messages for your group — event reminders, RSVP requests, volunteer sign-ups, carpool coordination, and scheduling notifications.

Other ways to join

You can also sign up on our online sign-up form or by completing a signed paper sign-up form. All three methods use the same consent language and disclosures.

Stopping messages and getting help

Reply STOP to any message to unsubscribe. You will receive one confirmation and then no further messages, unless you sign up again. Reply HELP for help, or contact us at nielsonpa@gmail.com or 801-921-9714.

Message and data rates may apply. Message frequency varies depending on group activities. Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.
