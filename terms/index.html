SMS Assistant text message coordination program · Last updated August 5, 2026

Program description

SMS Assistant is a text message (SMS) coordination program operated by Peter Nielson, an individual volunteer coordinator (sole proprietor), for small local groups he personally participates in — currently a religious congregation (the Seattle Hill Ward, including its Elders Quorum) and a family group. It sends informational and coordination messages to group members who have opted in. Messages include event reminders, RSVP requests, volunteer sign-ups, carpool coordination, and scheduling notifications. Every message identifies the group it relates to. This program does not send marketing or promotional messages.

How to opt in

You opt in by texting JOIN to (425) 534-5348 from the mobile number you want messages sent to. You may include a group code after the keyword, for example JOIN EQ. You will then be asked which group you are joining (if you did not say) and for your name, and you will receive a subscription confirmation message. Full details are on the text-to-join page.

Texting the program number is the only way to join. No message is sent to any number that has not first texted the program number to opt in. Phone numbers are never uploaded, purchased, imported, or enrolled by an administrator on someone's behalf. Your inbound opt-in text is retained as the record of your consent.

Consent to receive text messages is optional. It is not a condition of membership or of participating in any activity.

Message frequency

Message frequency varies depending on group activities.

Costs

Message and data rates may apply. These charges come from your mobile carrier and depend on your plan. SMS Assistant does not charge for this program.

Keywords

  • Join: JOIN, START, UNSTOP, YES
  • Cancel: STOP, STOPALL, UNSUBSCRIBE, CANCEL, END, QUIT
  • Help: HELP, INFO

How to opt out and get help

You can cancel at any time by replying STOP to any message. Opt-out is processed automatically and immediately. After you send STOP, you will receive one confirmation message and then no further messages, unless you sign up again.

For help, reply HELP to any message, or contact us at nielsonpa@gmail.com or 801-921-9714.

Carrier liability

Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.
