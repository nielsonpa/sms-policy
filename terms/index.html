SMS Coordination Program

Program description

The Seattle Hill Ward text message (SMS) program sends informational and coordination messages to congregation members who have opted in. Messages include event reminders, RSVP requests, volunteer sign-ups, carpool coordination, and scheduling notifications. This program does not send marketing or promotional messages.

How to opt in

You may opt in through our online sign-up form or by completing a paper sign-up form at a congregation meeting. Consent to receive text messages is not a condition of membership or of participating in any activity.

Message frequency

Message frequency varies depending on congregation activities.

Costs

Message and data rates may apply. These charges come from your mobile carrier and depend on your plan. The Seattle Hill Ward does not charge for this program.

How to opt out and get help

You can cancel at any time by replying STOP to any message. After you send STOP, you will receive one confirmation message and then no further messages, unless you sign up again.

For help, reply HELP to any message, or contact us at nielsonpa@gmail.com or 801-921-9714.

Carrier liability

Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.

Privacy

Your information is handled as described in our Privacy Policy.
