SMS Coordination Program

Program description

Personal Assistant Network (PAN) is a text message (SMS) coordination program operated by an individual volunteer coordinator for small local groups he personally participates in, currently a congregation (Seattle Hill Ward) and a family. It sends informational and coordination messages to group members who have opted in. Messages include event reminders, RSVP requests, volunteer sign-ups, carpool coordination, and scheduling notifications. Every message identifies the group it relates to. This program does not send marketing or promotional messages.

How to opt in

You may opt in through our online sign-up form, by completing a signed paper sign-up form, or by texting our opt-in keyword to our number. Consent to receive text messages is not a condition of membership or of participating in any activity.

Message frequency

Message frequency varies depending on group activities.

Costs

Message and data rates may apply. These charges come from your mobile carrier and depend on your plan. PAN does not charge for this program.

How to opt out and get help

You can cancel at any time by replying STOP to any message. After you send STOP, you will receive one confirmation message and then no further messages, unless you sign up again.

For help, reply HELP to any message, or contact us at nielsonpa@gmail.com or 801-921-9714.

Carrier liability

Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.

Privacy

Your information is handled as described in our Privacy Policy.
