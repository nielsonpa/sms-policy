Privacy Policy

SMS Assistant text message coordination program · Last updated August 7, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how SMS Assistant (“we,” “us”) — a text message coordination program operated by Peter Nielson, an individual volunteer coordinator (sole proprietor), for small local groups such as a religious congregation and a family — collects and uses information in connection with our text message (SMS) coordination program.

Information we collect

We collect your mobile phone number, the group you are joining, and the name you give us. We collect this information only when you provide it to us by text message: your number comes from the text you send to opt in, and your group and name come from your replies to our follow-up questions. We do not collect phone numbers from any other source — numbers are never uploaded, purchased, imported, or entered by an administrator on someone's behalf.

How we use your information

We use your name and mobile number solely to send you informational and coordination text messages about your group's activities — including event reminders, RSVP requests, volunteer sign-ups, carpool coordination, and scheduling notifications — and to record your replies, such as an RSVP or a volunteer sign-up. We do not send marketing or promotional messages.

How we share your information

Mobile information will not be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Text messaging originator opt-in data and consent will not be shared with any third parties. You can opt out of receiving text messages at any time by replying STOP.

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. We may share information only where required to operate the messaging service (for example, our messaging provider that delivers the texts) or where required by law.

Sharing contact details within your own group

Some groups use the program to coordinate carpools. Your group's leader decides whether contact sharing is available in that group at all; it is off unless the leader turns it on. If it is on and you offer to drive, we will ask you first whether your phone number and vehicle description may be included in the message sent to other members of that group, and we include them only if you reply YES. We ask each time you offer, your answer is never assumed from a previous offer, and you can tell us at any time to stop including them. These details are only ever shared with members of your own group inside this program — never with anyone outside it, and never for marketing.

How long we keep it

We retain your inbound opt-in text message as the record of your consent, along with your name, number, and group, for as long as you are subscribed. If you opt out, we retain the record of your opt-out so that we do not message you again.

Message frequency and rates

Message frequency varies depending on group activities. Message and data rates may apply, depending on your mobile carrier and plan.

Opting out

You can opt out at any time by replying STOP to any message. Opt-out is processed automatically and immediately. You will receive one confirmation message and then no further messages, unless you sign up again. For help, reply HELP or contact us using the details below.

Contact us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, contact us at nielsonpa@gmail.com or 801-921-9714.
