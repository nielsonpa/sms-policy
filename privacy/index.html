Privacy Policy

SMS Coordination Program

This Privacy Policy explains how the Seattle Hill Ward (“we,” “us”) collects and uses information in connection with our text message (SMS) coordination program.

Information we collect

When you sign up for our text messages, we collect your name and mobile phone number. We collect this information only when you provide it to us through our online sign-up form or a paper sign-up form.

How we use your information

We use your name and mobile number solely to send you informational and coordination text messages about congregation activities — including event reminders, RSVP requests, volunteer sign-ups, carpool coordination, and scheduling notifications. We do not send marketing or promotional messages.

How we share your information

Mobile information will not be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Text messaging originator opt-in data and consent will not be shared with any third parties. You can opt out of receiving text messages at any time by replying STOP.

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. We may share information only where required to operate the messaging service (for example, our messaging provider that delivers the texts) or where required by law.

Message frequency and rates

Message frequency varies depending on congregation activities. Message and data rates may apply, depending on your mobile carrier and plan.

Opting out

You can opt out at any time by replying STOP to any message. You will receive one confirmation message and then no further messages, unless you sign up again. For help, reply HELP or contact us using the details below.

Contact us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, contact us at nielsonpa@gmail.com or 801-921-9714.
