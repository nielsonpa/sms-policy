SMS Assistant

Get group texts: event reminders, RSVPs, volunteer sign-ups, carpools

This is the handout distributed at in-person meetings. There is no form to fill out — you join by texting.

Text JOIN to (425) 534-5348

from the mobile number you want messages sent to

What happens next

  1. You are asked which group you are joining (or add the group code yourself — for example, text JOIN EQ).
  2. You are asked for your name.
  3. You get a confirmation text, and then coordination messages for your group.

By texting JOIN you agree to receive informational and coordination text messages from SMS Assistant, operated by Peter Nielson. Msg frequency varies. Msg & data rates may apply. Reply STOP to cancel, HELP for help.

Consent is optional. It is not required to attend activities or to be a member of any group. We never add a number without a text from that number.
